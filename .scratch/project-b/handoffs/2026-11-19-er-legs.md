## What caused the kink

The old map assigned the shin's model rotation to every nearby bone at the knee. On HD Human female, bones 25 and 26 share a parent and pivot. In the supplied stock Run samples, 26 flexes 20–103° about the knee hinge. Bone 25 twists 3–8° about the length of the thigh. They skin different parts of the leg. At the ankle, 32 has a 0° stock Run rotation while its colocated child 40 articulates the foot. A landmark-only test sees the position of the knee and ankle through both errors.

The solver now drives the hip, the knee on the path to the toe, and the foot bone that carries the toe. Other bones keep their stock local transforms and inherit their solved parent's motion. This preserves the parallel thigh twists, ankle helpers, toe flexion and the classic shin's child helpers. On Tauren, the IK uses the current knee-to-hoof vector through the animated hock. The runtime uses no race-specific indices.

## Other animation changes

- Walk, run and sprint carry their fraction of a stride across clip lengths. Remote gait transitions use the same eased 180 ms minimum as local transitions, with a separate clock for blending into a late relay.
- A movement-cancelled melee attack gives the legs back to locomotion on a short crossfade. The torso continues on the combat playback clock through the remaining clip recovery, easing out at its end. The gait reads the controller's current transform.
- Draw and stow use the stock masked animation on each active arm. The stance follows the visible item's placement on its attachment key. A drawn two-handed weapon selects its kit bank for both idle and guard. Moving guard blends over the gait. The landed stock-clavicle behavior is retained.

## Verification

The installed-data fixture reads the M2 and its companion skin from each manifest. It compares four-weight vertex positions on helper-influenced mesh entries with stock-local deformation below independently identified hip, knee and foot landmarks. It exercises idle, guard, walk, two run frames, crouch and forward roll on both sides.

On the original solver (`895f4078`), Firna's HD Human female mesh had up to 0.096766 yd of helper displacement at idle and 0.279983 / 0.299697 yd on alternating run steps. The largest error across all eight rigs was 0.377414 yd on the crouching HD Orc male. The fixture failed its 0.0001 yd limit on the original solver. In the first corrected run, all 56 samples printed 0.000000 yd. The HD Human female comparison covered 1,871 helper-influenced mesh entries per frame. Her idle soles were 0.001 and 0.000 yd above ground, and the support foot on each run step was 0.003 / 0.004 yd above ground.

On the pushed model code (`abc446da`), `cargo fmt`, `cargo check -p benilla-app` and `cargo clippy -p benilla-app` passed. The two Clippy findings in `retarget.rs` were already present at `895f4078`. The named retarget tests passed (nine). The separate installed fixture passed again on all eight rigs, with the same 56 zero-error samples and the stock idle clavicles on HD Human female. Cargo compilation used sccache.

In [model CI](https://github.com/Dicetar/RustWrath/actions/runs/44520530673), Windows passed its build and tests. The Linux runner could not download `libasound2-dev` or `libudev-dev` from Ubuntu. Its setup stopped before installing sccache, so the subsequent live-code check could not execute the compiler wrapper.

## Owner's in-game check

### Heavy attack follow-up

On HD Human female, the axe attaches to bone 210 below helper 115 and wrist 62. The socket's own attachment offset is effectively zero. Stock Attack2HTight (18), variation 1, translates helper 115 from approximately 0.09 yd away from the wrist to 0.81 yd mid-swing. The other variation keeps it near the palm. The previous solver used ER wrist and weapon rotations but passed this independent stock translation through to the equipped item.

The ER pass now reads each rig's stock Stand translations along the short chain from wrist to socket. It uses those placements while the ER pose drives the hand, and still takes the weapon orientation from the attack. Local players and relayed peers share the fix. With Firna's axe drawn, repeat the two-handed heavy through windup, impact and recovery. The haft should stay inside her gripping hand.

### Legs and upper body

Enable Souls with F10 and set `retarget = true` and `retarget_locomotion = true` in `benilla-config/combat/souls.toml`. Start on wm-hd Human female with the dagger drawn, then compare Human male, Orc male and Tauren male on both base and wm-hd.

- Side-on run, `a000_020100`: follow the lower thigh into the knee on alternate strides. It should keep its contour as the shin bends. The boot should lift with the swing and meet the ground on the support step.
- Side-on idle, `a000_000000`: the lower thigh should remain continuous with the upper thigh, the knees should support the crouched stance, and the soles should rest on the ground. Check the shoulders from the front as well.
- Walk into a run, swing, then hold forward through the movement-cancel window. Look for immediate stepping under a finishing swing. Draw and stow once standing and once jogging. Toggle the dagger to two hands, then guard both standing and moving. Compare a relayed peer's gait transition and knees. Visible motion and equipment appearance require the owner's client.
